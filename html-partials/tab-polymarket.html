Polymarket Entry Touch Fillability
Estimate how many current BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, SOLUSDT, or XRPUSDT 5m executed trades would have touched a custom Polymarket entry price. Long trades use YES prices. Short trades use implied NO prices = 1 - YES. The panel prefers raw Polymarket prices-history extrema and falls back to synced checkpoints only when raw history is unavailable. This is a touch-based estimate, not an exact filled-order count.
