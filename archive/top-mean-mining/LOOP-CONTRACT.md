# TOP_MEAN Mining Campaign Loop Contract

Campaign ID: `TM-L1-C1`

This contract governs the approved TOP_MEAN mining campaign. The checker
reference and command examples remain in [`MINING-GUIDE.md`](./MINING-GUIDE.md).

## Contract version

- v1.0 governs B0-B1.
- v1.1 is effective before Batch 2 causal screening.
- Existing log records and discovery counts are not reclassified.

- Ledger: `archive/batch-open-score/sp500_top_mean_1788443592188_cgd3`
- Fingerprint: `ec81193124e572237acdcd826c6fe688da3adf2156e2abac17052a1c1838b887`
- All thresholds in this contract are checker `PRIMARY` percentage points.

## C1. Verdict labels

Per evaluation, the labels are unchanged:

- `EDGE` requires `PRIMARY >= +0.30pp`, event keep `>= 5%`, and the `CI95`
  lower bound `> 0`.
- Otherwise the result is `NO-EDGE`, `WORSE` (`PRIMARY < 0`), or
  `INCONCLUSIVE`.

`EDGE` is local discovery evidence only. It never authorizes engine changes or
validation.

## C2. Universal evidence checklist

Every promotion stage must pass all of these checks:

- `PRIMARY >=` the stage floor.
- `CI95` lower bound `> 0`.
- Event keep `>= 5%`.
- Dominant-exclusion `PRIMARY > 0`; the `EX_dominant` value itself must be
  positive.

There are no other sub-criteria.

## C3. Promotion gates

Promotion proceeds in this order:

1. **CAMPAIGN-QUALIFIED LEAD (discovery):** `PRIMARY >= +0.50pp` and all of
   C2.
2. **CORROBORATION:** a second rule in the same `familyKey`, with a different
   rule SHA and a different batch, also passes C3.1. Without corroboration
   there is no validation; batch 1 can never validate.
3. **L1 VALIDATION:** one human-authorized exact lead-rule SHA passes
   `PRIMARY >= +0.30pp` and all of C2 on validation. A validation `FAIL`
   retires the rule and its `familyKey`; validation cannot be tuned.
4. **L2 CONFIRMATION (C6):** the frozen rule passes `PRIMARY >= +0.30pp` and
   all of C2 on both L2 windows.
5. **AUDIT + HUMAN APPROVAL:** an auditor verifies the whole chain: log lines,
   rule SHA bytes, budget counters, checklist numbers, and both fingerprints;
   then a human approves.

## C4. L1 validation budget

- There are 30 metric-bearing views for L1 validation over the campaign
  lifetime.
- A view is spent when a valid `--window validation` invocation passes
  self-check and starts evaluating.
- Usage errors and self-check failures do not spend a view.
- There are at most 2 views per batch and at most 1 view per rule SHA.
- There is at most 1 statistically completed validation per `familyKey`.
- Accidental views count and contaminate the budget; they cannot be ignored.
- When 30 views are spent without a validated candidate, L1 ends.
- Before validation, the implementation agent appends a G2 authorization line
  with human approval. Unauthorized validation is an audit blocker.

## C5. Batch composition

Each batch must contain:

- 10 ideas.
- At least 6 distinct `familyKey` values.
- At most 2 ideas per `familyKey`.
- At most 1 identity/asset-list rule.
- At most 1 combination.

An `EDGE` inside a batch cannot spawn a same-batch variant.

## C6. L2 confirmation

An L2 bundle is designated only after the first L1 validation pass and must be
registered in `archive/top-mean-mining/L2-REGISTRATION.md` before any outcomes
are viewed.

L2 must use:

- The same 136-asset catalog, with a hash equal to L1.
- The same strategy `body_direction_placement_coherence`, with normalized
  `coherenceThreshold 0.705`.
- The same 4h interval, horizon `[24]`, long-side contract, costs, execution
  settings, sample start, and research constants.
- A pair list generated by `generateBalancedPairList`, algorithm
  `seeded_round_robin_v1`, `maxPairs 5555`, seed `2`.
- Pair-set and execution-order hashes that differ from L1.
- Canonical relationship-set Jaccard `<= 0.80`.
- A `COMPLETE` archive. An L1 `DATA_INCOMPLETE` exception does not extend to
  L2.

L2 has its own self-check before the candidate rule. If seed-2 structural
checks fail, stop and revise the registration; never try successive seeds
after results exist. There are at most 3 L2 bundles per campaign.

L2 provides pair-graph robustness; L1 validation provides the temporal
holdout.

## C7. Combination rules

A combination requires exactly two parents. Each parent must pass C3.1, the
parents must have different `familyKey` values and distinct causal sources,
both must pass the C2 concentration checklist, and neither parent may itself
be a combination.

The combination is a fresh idea with a new Q id, SHA, discovery count,
`familyKey`, and `parents=Qx+Qy`. It inherits no evidence. A human must
approve it at the scheduled campaign review.

## C8. Reviews and stopping

Review the campaign after batches 5, 10, 15, and 20. Each review chooses one
of: continue, refocus, or stop.

Stop for futility at a review when the preceding 5 batches contain no `EDGE`
and no corroborated pair.

Hard stop after any of the following:

- 20 batches (approximately 201 discovery evaluations including Q1).
- L1 validation budget exhaustion without a candidate.
- 3 failed L2 bundles.

`DONE-SUCCESS` requires the full C3 chain, engine-arm tests, and fresh-
coordinator parity. The checker label `EDGE` alone never means `DONE`.

## C8.1. Causal idea preflight

Every idea needs a passing discovery-window `DEGENERACY SCREEN` before receiving
a Q id or an outcome-bearing I2 record. The screen reads only metadata and
snapshots and never outcomes.

`ZERO` candidates are rejected and replaced. At most one `THIN` candidate may
appear in a final batch, with explicit human approval. `THIN` is advisory, not
an accounting exemption.

A causal screen does not increment `N_D` or the approximately 201 discovery cap.
`N_D` counts unique rule SHAs receiving outcome-bearing I2 records. Running
discovery before or despite the screen counts and cannot be exempted.

Generate and screen a 30-idea pool, then freeze and human-approve the final 10.
C5 and the 20-batch/approximately-201 caps are unchanged.

## C9. Endgame and promotion boundary

Promotion may add only a research selector arm in the shared OPEN_SCORE replay
engine, following the `pickMax -> appendSelection -> buildComparison ->
comparisonLine` path. It must also include `<ARM>_VS_TOP_MEAN`, `<ARM> selected
assets`, and `<ARM>_EX_<dominant>`.

Promotion must not touch current/latest selection, live or paper baskets,
Execution Lab, orders, workers, alerts, or allocation.

A promotion PR updates:

- `tests/batch-open-score-usd-replay-engine.spec.ts`
- `tests/batch-open-score-usd-max-active.spec.ts`

It must pass typecheck and both specs, update OPEN_SCORE documentation, and
record fresh-coordinator parity: overlap event IDs have exact assets and
returns within `1e-12`.

## C10. Re-baselining

L1 stops representing the production question if any of these change:

- Strategy or normalized parameters.
- Interval, horizon, direction, execution, or costs.
- Candidate eligibility, score, tie, block, bootstrap, or schema.
- Pair universe.
- Catalog membership or hash.
- Data source.
- Discovery or validation fences.

A successor ledger must be registered in the same manner as L2, with identity
recorded before outcomes. Carry forward the log, rule hashes, family lineage,
and global discovery count; reset standings per surface.

## C12. Roles

- The idea agent reads `idea-log.txt` directly; there is no standings artifact.
- A human approves the complete ideas JSON.
- The implementation agent implements exact rules in `DISCOVERY` only, with
  one I2 line per attempt.
- The auditor verifies hashes, verdicts, families, composition, and budget.
- Validation requires a human-approved G2 line before the run.
- The auditor re-engages before promotion and at every review.

## Batch procedure

1. Read `idea-log.txt` directly, freeze the approved 10-idea JSON, and verify
   C5 before implementation.
2. For each idea, implement the exact rule, run the mandatory checker
   self-check and discovery window, and append exactly one I2 result line.
3. Apply C1 and the full C2 checklist. Flag only C3.1 campaign-qualified
   leads and record corroboration candidates; do not validate without a
   human-approved G2 line.
4. At review points, run the C8 decision and re-engage the auditor. If an L1
   validation is authorized, append G2 first and append its sealed V2 result
   afterward. Never tune a validation failure.
5. Register an L2 identity in `L2-REGISTRATION.md` before viewing outcomes,
   then enforce C6 and append the applicable sealed records.
6. Finish only when C8 `DONE-SUCCESS` requirements and the C9 audit and human
   approval requirements are satisfied.
